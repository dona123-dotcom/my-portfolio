06 / Integrated final challenge

Connect the signals.

A university receives suspicious targeted emails. Shortly afterward, several computers become inaccessible, and the university website experiences service disruptions.

Write your analysis before revealing the model response.

Model analysis · Compare with your response
  • Threat categories: Targeted emails suggest spear phishing and possibly pretexting. Inaccessible computers may indicate ransomware; website disruption is consistent with a DoS or DDoS event. These are hypotheses, not proof of a shared cause.
  • Impact: Loss of access to teaching and research systems, interrupted public services, recovery costs, potential data exposure, and reputational harm.
  • Immediate priorities: Activate incident response; preserve evidence; isolate affected endpoints; verify the scope of encryption and service disruption; coordinate with IT, leadership, and relevant providers; communicate through trusted channels. Avoid assuming the website issue has the same cause.
  • Long-term measures: Targeted-email training, MFA, patching, email verification, tested protected backups, monitoring, traffic mitigation, and rehearsed response plans.

A strong analysis distinguishes observations from inferences and validates each hypothesis with evidence.
